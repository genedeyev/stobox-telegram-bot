# Stobox Intelligence

Source: https://www.stobox.io/llms-full.txt, read on 27 September 2026.

Stobox Intelligence turns a company into one canonical, verifiable record and
scores it. It is the first of three products: Intelligence (the verified company
record), Raisable (the offering, through licensed broker-dealer partners) and
Compass (token issuance). Page: https://www.stobox.io/intelligence.

Prices are published and flat: https://www.stobox.io/pricing. A company can
check its own number first with the readiness score:
https://www.stobox.io/readiness.

For anything beyond this, describe Intelligence only as the live page does.
